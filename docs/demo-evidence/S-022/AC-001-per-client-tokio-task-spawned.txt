WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES:
  The daemon's accept loop spawns a dedicated Tokio task for each connecting TUI client
  and registers it in the SubscriberList (len goes 0 -> 1 after connect). When the client
  drops its UnixStream (simulating TUI EOF), the per-client task detects IpcError::Disconnected
  and calls remove_subscriber, draining the SubscriberList back to 0. Both transitions are
  asserted with 2-second polling deadlines, proving the lifecycle is deterministic.
