WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES:
  Two fully independent TUI client connections are spawned concurrently via tokio::join!.
  Each client connects, consumes InitialState (required before daemon reads from client),
  then sends PermissionDecision for the same prompt_id. The observer subscriber asserts
  exactly 1 PermissionPromptResolved arrives within 500ms (the first decision won the
  race). A second recv times out after 100ms, confirming no duplicate Resolved was
  broadcast. This is the production-grade multi-client scenario: the oneshot::channel
  and PendingDecisionRegistry together enforce at-most-one resolution even under
  concurrent pressure.
